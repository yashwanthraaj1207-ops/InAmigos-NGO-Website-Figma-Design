# 🎨 InAmigos NGO Website UI/UX Design Case Study

A high-fidelity NGO website design created in Figma to improve donor engagement, volunteer participation, transparency, and community impact visibility.

---

## 🌟 Project Overview

This project was completed during my AI Web Developer Internship at InAmigos Foundation.

The goal was to redesign and modernize the NGO's digital experience through a user-centered approach, making it easier for donors, volunteers, and beneficiaries to interact with the organization.

---

## 🚨 Problem Statement

Many NGO websites face challenges such as:

* Low donor engagement
* Limited transparency
* Poor information hierarchy
* Weak volunteer onboarding experience
* Lack of impact visualization

This design addresses these challenges through a modern and intuitive user experience.

---

## 🎯 Design Goals

* Increase donor trust and transparency
* Improve volunteer registration flow
* Showcase measurable social impact
* Create a responsive user experience
* Improve information accessibility
* Strengthen NGO credibility

## ✨ Key Features

### Hero Section

Mission-focused landing page with clear call-to-action buttons.

### Impact Statistics

Highlights lives impacted, campaigns completed, and community reach.

### National Community Trust

Builds donor confidence through trust indicators and credibility metrics.

### Campaigns & Local Projects

Displays ongoing initiatives and fundraising campaigns.

### Donation Impact Tracker

Shows how donations create measurable change.

### Lifecycle of Giving

Visual representation of donation collection and utilization.

### Transformation Chronicles

Success stories and beneficiary experiences.

### Media Gallery

Photos and videos showcasing NGO activities.

### Transparency Center

Reports, accountability metrics, and financial transparency.

### Volunteer Registration

Simple onboarding experience for volunteers.

### Transformation Voices

Testimonials from beneficiaries, volunteers, and donors.
